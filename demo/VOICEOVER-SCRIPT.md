# NinjaParent — Demo Video Voiceover Script (~90 sec)

Use this script when presenting live or recording a narrated version over `demo/ninjaparent-demo.mp4`.

---

**[0:00 – 0:12] Opening — Dashboard overview**

> "Meet NinjaParent — the command center for busy parents juggling school life across multiple kids and apps. Sarah has three children at two schools. Gmail, Spider, Parent Pay, and SIMS — all connected, all syncing."

---

**[0:12 – 0:28] AI Priority Insight**

> "Instead of 47 unread emails, she sees one prioritized queue. Our AI analyzes every message — deadlines, payments, registrations — and ranks what needs attention right now. Trip payment due tomorrow? That's number one."

---

**[0:28 – 0:45] Action cards scroll**

> "Every card is actionable. Pay now. Register interest. Sign the permission slip. Each shows the child, the source app, the deadline, and why it's prioritized. No more hunting through newsletters for buried deadlines."

---

**[0:45 – 0:58] Filter by child — Noah**

> "Filter by child instantly. Here's everything for Noah — his overdue maths homework, the science museum trip payment, parents' evening booking. One click, one view."

---

**[0:58 – 1:12] Filter by type — Payments, Homework, Overdue**

> "Filter by type — payments only, homework only, overdue items. Batch your Parent Pay tasks. Clear the homework queue. NinjaParent adapts to how you work."

---

**[1:12 – 1:25] Complete action + Lily filter**

> "Mark items complete as you go. Switch to Lily — coding club registration closing Friday, spellings due tomorrow. Every child, every school, one dashboard."

---

**[1:25 – 1:30] Close**

> "NinjaParent. School life, unified. Never miss what matters."

---

## Tips for recording

- Run the prototype live at `localhost:5173` for investor meetings
- Or play `demo/ninjaparent-demo.mp4` with this script
- Re-record anytime: `cd ninjaparent && npm run demo:record`
